1. Website Structure
Your portfolio website will have the following pages:
✅ Home Page – Introduction with an eye-catching hero section.
✅ About Me Page – Your background, skills, and experience.
✅ My Projects Page – A showcase of your video editing work using YouTube embeds.
✅ Contact Me Page – A simple form for inquiries and social media links.

2. Home Page (Landing Page)
Hero Section:

A large banner introducing you as a Professional Video Editor (like in the attached image).

A background animation (e.g., floating Premiere Pro & After Effects icons with glowing effects).

A CTA button like “Let’s Talk” leading to the Contact page.

Features Section:

Showcase your skills in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and other tools.

Animated icons/logos of the software you use.

Smooth hover effects when users interact with elements.

Why Choose Me Section:

Display your unique editing style, past client testimonials, and project highlights.

Use a card flip animation to reveal details about each service you offer.

Scroll Animations:

Text and images will fade in and move dynamically as users scroll.

Hover animations on buttons and interactive elements for a smooth UX.

3. About Me Page
A brief introduction about you, your journey, and your experience in video editing.

Add a high-quality image of yourself with an overlay effect when hovered.

Animated statistics (e.g., Years of Experience, Projects Completed, Happy Clients).

4. My Projects Page
This page will feature all the videos you’ve edited, displayed using a YouTube viewer for easy playback.

Video Display Format:

A sideways sliding carousel for featured projects.

A grid layout below for all projects, each with a hover effect.

Users can click on a video to play it directly on the website.


Hover effect on thumbnails (e.g., a red glow or zoom-in effect).

5. Contact Me Page
A contact form with fields for Name, Email, Message (integrated with an API like Formspree or Nodemailer).


A CTA section encouraging visitors to get in touch for collaborations.

Background glowing effect or a subtle animated gradient to make the section stand out.

6. Animations & Effects to Implement
✅ Smooth Scroll Animations: Elements fade in, slide, or zoom in as users scroll.
✅ Hover Effects: Buttons, images, and text change color, glow, or slightly enlarge.
✅ Floating Elements: Video editing software icons floating on the homepage.
✅ Dark Mode & Light Mode Toggle (Optional) for a modern experience.
✅ Parallax Effect on Backgrounds to add depth to the page.

7. Responsive Design & Optimization
Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Lazy loading for images and videos to improve performance.

SEO optimization with meta tags, Open Graph (OG) images for social sharing.

Fast-loading pages using Next.js optimizations (server-side rendering, image optimization)
